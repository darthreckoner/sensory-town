# Reference notes for the v2 side-by-side review

Web search turned up guides and descriptions but no usable reference screenshots. So the "reference board" judgments compare our screenshots against these documented traits, not against images. Treat them accordingly.

## Roblox — Dress to Impress (fashion, ages ~9–14)

**Loop:**
- Themed style rounds (streetwear, high glam, spooky…).
- A runway walk with poses at the end.
- Voting on creativity, theme fit and style.
- A pose menu (pink button) with a pose preview before committing.

**Look:**
- Kotaku describes big-headed femme characters with "bedroom eyes", big lips, and trendy/skimpy outfits.
- It compares the aesthetic to a 2000s Bratz game.
- The age signal is **glam / attitude**, not head size: stylized-big heads are fine if the face and pose read glam, not kawaii/baby.

**Fashion cues players chase:**
- TikTok fits, Y2K revival, "BRAT" green, layered statement pieces.
- A 2–3 color palette per look.

**Takeaway for Style:** poses with attitude, lashes and lips, trendy layered fits, and a photo/runway moment.

## Roblox — Welcome to Bloxburg (building)

- Modern builds are minimalist and functional: clean lines, neutral palettes, a few personal decor pieces.
- Dark-modern builds layer ambient, task and accent lighting.
- Popular formats: 1-story / 2-story modern, "blush modern", aesthetic bedrooms. Community builds are judged on interiors.

**Takeaway for Build:**
- Neutral walls with accent decor.
- Emissive lamps and LED strips (accent lighting).
- Two floors.
- Interiors worth decorating.

## Wax-cracking squishies (the real toys + ASMR channels)

- Retail wax-cracking squishies are often **pastel** (donut, heart, ball, paw), with a hard wax shell that cracks on the first squeeze. Pastel on the *object* is authentic, not babyish — the studio around it carries the tone.
- ASMR channels (e.g. "ASMR Emiko Ffujio") post wax-cracking slime: wax beads cracking slime, wax snow slime, "cranky wax cracking". There are also clay-shell toys with squishies inside (Oddly Labs).
- Close-up audio of the crack and squeeze is the content.

**Takeaway for the Studio:**
- Keep pastel wax as an option.
- Shapes worth adding later: ball, paw, donut. A donut needs a non-radial mesh.
- Wax-bead slime is a known format; it's close to our "crunchy".
