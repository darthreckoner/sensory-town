# The real test: 15 minutes with the kids (and an fps check)

All of my grades come from a desktop browser with scripted touch. Only two things can actually confirm the game works:
- your kids playing it on the iPads,
- the iPads' own frame rate.

## Before they play (2 min, on each iPad)

1. **Install:** open the link in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Launch it from the icon once while on Wi-Fi.
2. **Check offline:**
   1. Turn on **Airplane Mode**.
   2. Swipe the app closed.
   3. Reopen it from the icon. It should load.
3. **Check sound:** turn **silent mode off**, volume about half.
4. **Check frame rate (no Mac needed):**
   1. In the game, tap the screen with **three fingers at once**. A small readout appears top-right: `fps`, `scene`, `dpr`, `calls`.
   2. Drive around Downtown for 20 seconds, then read `fps`.
     - **≥ 45:** good.
     - **30–45:** playable. The game lowers resolution by itself; `dpr` dropping from 1.5 toward 1 is that safety net at work.
     - **< 30:** tell me the iPad model.
   3. Three-finger tap again to hide the readout.
   (If you have a Mac, Safari → Develop → [iPad] → Timelines gives exact frame times. It isn't needed.)

## The session (15 min, per kid, ideally separately)

**Say only:** "This is a game I helped make. Play whatever you want — I'm just going to watch." Then stay quiet. Don't explain or point, and don't answer "how do I…?" unless they're stuck for more than a minute (note that moment; it's the most useful data).

**Write down** (a phone note is fine):

| Minute | What they're doing | Did they get stuck? Where? | What they said |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | | | |
| 2–5 | | | |
| 5–10 | | | |
| 10–15 | | | |

**Watch for:**
- **The drop:** do they jump from the party bus and steer the glider without help?
- **Building:** do they find 🔨 and build something (walls, a ramp) unaided? How long did it take? Do they put furniture inside?
- **Locker:** do they change skin, back bling or glider? Do they notice the 💎 charm counter and chase charms for unlocks?
- **Emotes:** do they use the 😀 wheel? Do they laugh at a dance?
- **Rides:** which ride do they pick? Do they use 🚗 to call it?
- **Studio:** do they visit the Studio and crack wax more than 3 times without being asked?
- **Words that matter:** "creepy", "babyish", "boring", "this is like Fortnite", "can I keep playing".
- **Music:** do they turn it off?

## Pass / fail

- **PASS:** they build something unaided, change their look in the Locker, and nobody says "creepy" or "babyish"; at 15 minutes they want to keep going.
- **FAIL:** any one of those misses. Send me your notes — the stuck moments go straight into the next round.

## Two-iPad town swap (optional, 3 min)

1. iPad A: ☰ → **Share town** → AirDrop to iPad B → **Save to Files**.
2. iPad B: ☰ → **Import town** → pick the file → **Yes, replace**.
3. Both kids should now see both houses.
