## Round 3 (designer: "more bugs than a roach motel" + hair accessories, stuck in water, lost car)
Testing method changed: instead of scripted spots, `window.__worldcheck` (src/core/worldcheck.ts, dev / `?selftest`) sweeps the whole map against the rendered geometry:
- `feet(step, tol, area?)` puts the walker on a grid and raycasts the drawn meshes. It flags SUNK (a surface between ankles and head, foliage excluded) and FLOAT (no surface under the feet).
- `camera(mode, step, area?)` tests 4 headings per spot and flags any mesh in the first third of the lens→player line.
- `probe(x, z)` lists the hits at one spot.
- `houses()` flags roof pieces with nothing under them and pieces outside the plot.

Root causes found and fixed:
- **Skate plaza pavement** was draped over the ramps on a 6 m grid, floating up to 0.35 m above them ("sunk in the road"). Roads and plazas now use `paveHeight`, which never drapes over stunts.
- **Walker vs ramp mesh:** walkers stood on the smooth math curve, but the ramp mesh is drawn as 0.75 m blocks ("inside a ramp"). Walkers now stand on the drawn cells (`hFoot`). The car still rides the smooth curve.
- **Terrain physics** used bilinear interpolation on a grid twice as fine as the drawn mesh, so feet drifted up to 0.8 m off the ground on hills. `terrainHeight` now interpolates the drawn triangles exactly.
- **Wide road ribbons** were one flat chord across a cross-slope. They're now split into ~1.5 m lanes.
- **Mall stair landing** was walkable thin air. It now has a slab, posts and a rail; roof and landing decks match the railings, and walkers can't stroll off railed decks or stairs.
- **Walker capsule** was probed at its centre, so it stopped half inside walls. It's now probed at the leading edge.
- **Camera** only checked the ground under the lens. It now rises until its sightline to the player clears ramps and hills.
- **Home Hills gate sign** back was a black slab; it's now a lit "DOWNTOWN" face. The skate hoop is lowered so its bottom tube is buried.

Feet sweep: 489 bad spots out of 2,354 → 8 one-offs (props without colliders). Camera sweep (foot): 21 obstructions out of ~4,500 views, plus foliage.

## Assumptions made (Director's calls, need designer review)
- "Build together" = both profiles build in one shared town on the same iPad; the two iPads swap towns via export/import files. No live sync (offline, no server).
- Import replaces the entire town **and** all profiles on the receiving iPad (simplest mental model: "now both iPads have the same town"). This iPad's sound settings are kept.
- After picking a profile the game opens Dress Up first (matches the session loop: dress → drive → build/workshop); "Let's go!" drives off.
- Car color is chosen from a 🎨 button in the Drive scene (it's where the car is).
- Wax coating happens with the squishy hanging from a candy stick over the pot (dip = drag it down); cracking happens on a plate.
- Wax hardens automatically after each layer (`tuning.wax.hardenTime` = 4 s); ❄️ Cool speeds it up 4×. Cracking is blocked (with a hint) until every layer is hard.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages via Actions (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`), `BASE=/<repo>/`.
- Fonts: system rounded font (`ui-rounded` = SF Pro Rounded on iPad) — no web fonts, fully offline.
